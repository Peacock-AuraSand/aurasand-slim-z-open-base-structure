# COMMERCIAL_USE / 商业使用说明

## 中文条款

### 需要申请商业授权的情况

- 直接使用本项目 3DM 文件进行批量生产、销售或接单代工。
- 在本项目整体基础主体结构上轻微修改后进行商业销售。
- 以品牌名上架、商品化宣传、众筹、预售、团购或公司项目使用。
- 通过本资料或其轻微改版获得直接或间接商业利益。

### 不需要商业授权的情况

仅学习本项目中的结构思路、局部设计方法、孔位思路、推槽设计思路、底板凸起概念、底板纹路表达或装配启发，并将其用于独立设计的新产品，不需要商业授权。即使该独立设计的新产品后续商业化，也不因参考这些局部思路而需要向雀音匠申请授权。

### 费用与审核

商业用途需提前提交申请并获得雀音匠书面确认。申请入口为本项目 GitHub 组织页面展示的公开邮箱；申请人应在邮件中说明真实使用主体、联系方式、计划用途、销售渠道或品牌标识方式。现阶段，雀音匠对符合条件并通过审核的商业使用授权不收取授权费用。

该审核机制仅用于确认使用主体、使用方式及标识规范，防止仿冒、盗用品牌、混淆来源、误导消费者及不正当竞争行为。

### 可拒绝授权的情况

- 曾仿冒、侵权、盗图、冒用雀音匠或 Peacock 品牌。
- 存在恶意混淆消费者、不正当竞争或损害雀音匠权益的记录。
- 无法说明真实使用主体、销售渠道或品牌标识规范。
- 使用方式可能导致消费者误认为其产品为雀音匠官方产品、授权产品或联名产品。

## English Terms

### Commercial Use Requiring Approval

- Directly using the 3DM file for mass production, sales, or commissioned production.
- Making only minor changes to the overall base structure and then selling or commercializing the result.
- Listing the result under a brand name, using it in product marketing, crowdfunding, pre-orders, group buys, or company projects.
- Obtaining direct or indirect commercial benefit from these materials or a lightly modified version of them.

### Use That Does Not Require Commercial Approval

Learning from the structural ideas, local design methods, hole-layout concepts, slot-design concepts, raised-point concepts on the bottom plate, bottom-plate pattern expressions, or assembly inspiration from this project, and applying those ideas to an independently designed product, does not require commercial approval. Even if that independently designed product is later commercialized, no commercial approval from Peacock is required merely because those local ideas were referenced.

### Fees and Review

Commercial use requires an application and prior written approval from Peacock. Applications should be submitted to the public email address shown on this project’s GitHub organization page. The applicant should include the real user, contact information, intended use, sales channels, and brand-marking approach in the email. At this stage, approved commercial use is free of license fees.

The review mechanism is used to confirm the user, usage scenario, and marking rules, and to prevent counterfeiting, brand misuse, source confusion, consumer deception, and unfair competition.

### Grounds for Refusal

- Prior counterfeiting, infringement, image theft, or misuse of the 雀音匠 or Peacock brand.
- Records of consumer confusion, unfair competition, or harm to Peacock's rights and interests.
- Failure to identify the real user, sales channels, or brand-marking approach.
- Any use likely to make consumers believe that a third-party product is an official Peacock product, authorized product, or co-branded product.
