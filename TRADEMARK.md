# TRADEMARK / 商标与品牌声明

## 中文条款

### 不开放的品牌资产

- 雀音匠、Peacock 及相关品牌名称。
- Logo、图形标识、包装设计、宣传图片、商品命名体系、商业识别元素。
- 官方产品照片、公众号文章图片、宣传素材和未明确列入本项目的其他品牌资产。

### 禁止行为

- 不得使用雀音匠或 Peacock 品牌名、Logo、包装或宣传素材销售第三方产品。
- 不得暗示第三方产品为雀音匠官方产品、授权产品、联名产品或同源量产产品。
- 不得以容易造成消费者混淆的方式使用本项目资料、项目名称或品牌相关词汇。

### 允许的说明性引用

在遵守本项目规则的前提下，可以为说明资料来源而使用类似“参考 Peacock AuraSand SLIM-Z 开源基础主体结构资料”的描述，但不得将该描述用于暗示商业授权、官方背书或联名关系。

## English Terms

### Brand Assets Not Released

- The brand names 雀音匠, Peacock, and related brand names.
- Logos, graphic marks, packaging designs, promotional images, product naming systems, and other brand-identification assets.
- Official product photos, WeChat article images, promotional materials, and any other brand assets not expressly included in this project.

### Prohibited Use

- No third party may use the 雀音匠 or Peacock brand name, logo, packaging, or promotional materials to sell third-party products.
- No third party may imply that its product is an official Peacock product, authorized product, co-branded product, or product from the same production source.
- No third party may use this project's materials, project name, or brand-related terms in a way likely to confuse consumers.

### Permitted Descriptive Reference

Subject to compliance with this project's rules, a descriptive reference such as “referencing the Peacock AuraSand SLIM-Z open base-structure materials” may be used to identify the source of reference. Such wording may not be used to imply commercial authorization, official endorsement, or a co-branding relationship.
